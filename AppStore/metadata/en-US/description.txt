Open .eml and Outlook .msg files on your Mac. EML/MSG Viewer displays decoded message headers and HTML messages with inline images. When a message has no HTML body, it shows the plain text instead.

Open attachments from the attachments bar, or use Save All to save them to a folder you choose.

Choose light, dark, or system appearance, and adjust the font family and size. You can also set the app as the default viewer for .eml and .msg files.

The app is sandboxed. No account is required, and the app collects no data.
