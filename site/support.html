How do I set the app as the default viewer?

Open EML/MSG Viewer and choose “Set as default for .eml and .msg” on the welcome screen. You can then double-click those files in Finder.

Can I open Outlook .msg files?

Yes. Open a .msg file in EML/MSG Viewer without Outlook.

Where do attachments go?

Choose Save All in the attachments bar and select a destination folder. Select an individual attachment to open it in its default app.

Can messages load remote images?

Yes. HTML messages may request images from remote servers. Those servers may receive your IP address.
