Your privacy

Privacy policy

EML/MSG Viewer collects no data. The app requires no account and uses no analytics or tracking. It is sandboxed.

Remote images

When Load remote images is on, HTML messages may load images from their source servers. The setting is on by default. Those servers receive a network request and may receive your IP address. Turn the setting off to block all remote content.
